    Demo groups

    Made-up groups with months of history. Open one as its treasurer, or view it as any member.

    AI reads, people decide

    The AI only reads receipts and messages. Fixed rules do the maths, and a person always makes the final call.

    No self-approval

    The person who records an entry can never confirm it. That includes the treasurer.

    History you can check

    Each action is sealed to the one before it. If anyone edits the past, the integrity check fails.

    A record, not a score

    Members get a factual payment record. No hidden scores, no automated decisions about people.
